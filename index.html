The Ionian Steps

Three floors, three keys: C, then G, then D, one step around the circle of fifths each time. Each floor adds something new. Set up your voice or instrument, then go in.

01Microphone

Headphones give the cleanest result. Sound from a speaker can reach the mic, and phones often play the game quietly once the mic is on.
